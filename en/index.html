About us

A publisher built from academia

EPIC (Ediciones, Publicaciones e Investigaciones Científicas) is an Ecuadorian academic publisher that offers researchers and lecturers a professional and transparent way to publish.

We support the whole process: from the manuscript to a published, formally registered book.

Mission

To disseminate scientific and academic knowledge through open-access books, with editorial rigor, peer review and formal registration with ISBN and DOI.

Vision

To be a leading Ecuadorian academic publisher for the quality, visibility and reach of its publications.

Services

What we do for your work

A comprehensive editorial service with academic standards.

Peer review

Evaluation by two anonymous external peers, using a double-blind methodology, to ensure the quality and impartiality of each work. You receive the reports and a verifiable peer-review certificate.

See policy →

Plagiarism check

We verify the originality of the manuscript before sending it to review.

Basic copyediting

One round of review of spelling, grammar and punctuation on the approved manuscript.

Layout and cover

Professional interior layout and cover design.

ISBN, DOI and rights registration

We manage the ISBN, the DOI (through Zenodo) and the copyright registration with SENADI.

Open-access publication

Your book is published on the EPIC website, and you receive an acceptance letter and a certificate with a verification QR code.

Ask about pricing

Payment by bank transfer to the company's account, with an electronic invoice.

Included

  • Preliminary evaluation and double-blind peer review by two external peers
  • Plagiarism check
  • Basic copyediting of the text (one round)
  • Layout and cover design
  • ISBN, barcode, DOI and copyright registration with SENADI
  • Digital open-access version, published on the EPIC website and on Zenodo
  • Acceptance letter, arbitration certificate and publication letter

Not included

  • Translation
  • Deep style editing or a second round of copyediting
  • Distribution in bookstores or fairs, or shipping of physical copies
  • Printing (we do not offer it at this time)

If peer review does not recommend publishing the work, the service is not completed and the author's cost is limited to a one-time processing fee, communicated before starting.

Editorial process

We support you from start to finish

From manuscript to published work, with the support of our editorial team.

1

Manuscript submission

You write to us with your proposal and we evaluate it editorially.

2

Initial review

We check the format and originality of the text.

3

Peer review and corrections

Two external peers evaluate your work and we work with you on the corrections.

4

Publication and registration

Your work is published with ISBN and DOI, in open access.

Estimated time: 30 to 45 days from delivery of the complete manuscript. It also depends on reviewer turnaround.
