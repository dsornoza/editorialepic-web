Editorial policies

How we evaluate, publish and look after each work. This is a summary of the essentials; if you need more detail, write to us at contacto@editorialepic.com.

In brief

  • Every work goes through double-blind peer review, with two external reviewers.
  • We check each manuscript with plagiarism-detection software before peer review.
  • Open access under a Creative Commons license: the author keeps their rights.
  • ISBN, DOI and copyright registration are included in the service.
  • Payment for the service does not influence the editorial decision.
  • We follow the COPE guidelines on publication ethics.

1. What we publish

EPIC publishes academic and research books in open access: research books, academic textbooks and manuals.

Works must be original and unpublished. We do not publish discriminatory or defamatory content, or content that incites violence, nor works that infringe third-party rights. Works already published by another publisher are accepted only if the author shows they are entitled to transfer them.

2. Double-blind peer review

  • Each work is evaluated by two external peers, anonymously: the reviewer does not know the author and the author does not know the reviewers. If their reports contradict each other, a third peer is consulted.
  • Reviewers are external to EPIC and to the author's institution, have no conflict of interest with the work and have a verifiable academic record (ORCID profile and related publications).
  • Those who edit or sit on the Editorial Board do not evaluate works as peers.
  • We evaluate originality and contribution, methodological rigor, theoretical grounding, structure and clarity, ethics and editorial fit.
  • The decision may be: accept, accept with minor changes, accept with major changes or reject, always with a written justification.
  • Manuscripts and reports are confidential. Authors receive the review reports without any data that identifies the reviewers.

Evidence for your academic evaluation. Each work is backed by the acceptance letter, the review reports from two external peers, the arbitration certificate, the ISBN, the DOI and the publication letter. The regulation on the career and ranking of academic staff in the higher-education system requires, for a book, verifiable review by at least two anonymous external peers and an ISBN or ISSN; our process is designed to generate that evidence. Whether a work is recognized depends on each institution's regulations, and EPIC cannot guarantee it.

3. Process and timelines

  1. Submission and formal check: form, complete manuscript and originality statement.
  2. Plagiarism check with Compilatio.
  3. Preliminary evaluation by the Editorial Board: relevance and minimum quality.
  4. Double-blind peer review by two external peers.
  5. Editorial decision and revisions: the author responds to the reports and submits the revised version.
  6. Production: basic copyediting, layout and cover design, with the author's final approval.
  7. Registration and publication: ISBN, DOI, copyright registration and publication on editorialepic.com and Zenodo.

Estimated time: 30 to 45 days from receipt of the complete manuscript. It depends on reviewer availability and on the author's response times; if any deadline is extended, we will let you know and explain why.

4. Ethics, plagiarism and corrections

  • Authorship: authors are those who made a substantial contribution and approve the final version. We ask for each author's contribution to be declared (CRediT taxonomy) and recommend the use of ORCID. Any change to the list of authors requires the written consent of all of them.
  • Unacceptable conduct: plagiarism, fabrication or falsification of data, fictitious authorship, duplicate publication, manipulation of peer review and failure to disclose conflicts of interest.
  • Plagiarism check: every manuscript is analyzed with Compilatio before peer review. The editor reviews the report and files it in the work's record.
  • If there is a suspicion: anyone may report it to contacto@editorialepic.com. The Editorial Board examines it with the people involved and resolves it following the COPE guidelines. Possible measures range from a correction to retraction or cancellation of the process.
  • Corrections and retractions: errors are corrected with a dated erratum. A retracted work remains visible with a notice and the reason, to preserve the academic record.

5. Artificial intelligence

  • Authors: may use AI tools as support (translation, writing, search), but must declare which tool they used and for what. They are responsible for the content and for verifying references; references or data invented by AI are a serious offense. Images generated with AI must be identified. An AI tool cannot be listed as an author.
  • Reviewers: must not upload manuscripts to external AI tools, because that breaches confidentiality.
  • EPIC: uses AI only as support and under human supervision. Editorial decisions are always human.

6. Independence and conflicts of interest

  • Payment is remuneration for editorial services, not for the acceptance of the work. The decision depends solely on the peers' reports and on the Editorial Board, who do not know whether the author has paid.
  • Authors, reviewers, editors and Board members declare any conflict of interest in writing; anyone who has one abstains from taking part.
  • To avoid editorial inbreeding, EPIC does not publish works by its manager or owner. Works by Board or team members are handled without their involvement, with external peer review and with the connection declared in the work.

7. Rights, licenses and open access

  • The author keeps their copyright. Through the publishing agreement, the author grants EPIC a non-exclusive license to edit, publish and distribute the work in digital format.
  • Default license: CC BY 4.0. Optionally, the author may choose CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Creative Commons licenses are irrevocable for copies already distributed.
  • The author is responsible for obtaining permissions for third-party images, tables or texts.
  • Identifiers and dissemination: each work receives an ISBN and a DOI (through Zenodo), is registered with SENADI and is published on editorialepic.com and on Zenodo, with title, abstract and keywords in Spanish and English.
  • We publish works in Spanish or English.

8. Service and terms

Single package per title No discounts. Write to us at contacto@editorialepic.com to find out the price.

Included:

  • Preliminary evaluation and double-blind peer review by two external peers
  • Plagiarism check with Compilatio
  • Basic copyediting (one round of spelling, grammar and punctuation)
  • Layout and cover design
  • ISBN, barcode, DOI and copyright registration with SENADI
  • Digital version and distribution on editorialepic.com and Zenodo
  • Acceptance letter, arbitration certificate and publication letter

Not included: translation, deep style editing or a second round of copyediting, distribution in bookstores or fairs, or shipping of physical copies. We do not offer printing at this time.

If peer review does not recommend publishing the work, the service is not completed and the author's cost is limited to a one-time processing fee, communicated before starting.

Payment is made by bank transfer to the company's account, with an electronic invoice.

9. Complaints, appeals and personal data

  • Complaints and appeals: if you disagree with a decision or with the treatment you received, write to us at contacto@editorialepic.com. An editorial decision can be appealed with arguments about the process or about the content of the reports; the appeal is examined by the Editorial Board with at least one member who did not take part in the original decision.
  • Personal data: we process the data of authors and reviewers only for editorial management, in accordance with Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection. Data that identifies reviewers is not given to authors. You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your data at contacto@editorialepic.com, except for what we must keep for legal reasons or for the integrity of the academic record.
