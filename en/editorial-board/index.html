Editorial Board

The Editorial Board safeguards the quality, independence and ethics of the works EPIC publishes. It decides which works are published in light of the reports from external peers, independently of the publisher's commercial interests.

What it does

  • Approves and reviews the editorial policies.
  • Decides whether a work goes to peer review after the preliminary evaluation.
  • Approves the evaluation criteria and the reviewer pool.
  • Decides on each work in light of the reports: accept, accept with changes or reject.
  • Resolves complaints, appeals and possible cases of misconduct.

Become an EPIC reviewer

We are looking for lecturers and researchers with a doctorate, or with a master's degree and relevant academic output, from any country, for the reviewer pool. Review is double-blind and you will receive a peer-review certificate with a verification code. We will publish the list of reviewers every year, without linking any of them to a specific work.
